# Code of Conduct

Ashmere Software wants technical collaboration to be professional, constructive and safe.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors, maintainers and community members should:

- discuss ideas and code on their merits;
- give actionable, technically specific feedback;
- assume good faith unless evidence shows otherwise;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and personal attacks;
- keep security disclosures within the appropriate private channel;
- accept that maintainers may reject changes for security, scope, quality or maintainability reasons.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following is not acceptable in Ashmere Software project spaces:

- harassment or intimidation;
- discriminatory or degrading remarks;
- deliberate disclosure of private information;
- publishing active vulnerability details against the security policy;
- malicious contributions, backdoors or deliberately deceptive code;
- repeated disruption after a maintainer has asked for it to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and may restrict participation where needed to protect contributors, users or projects.

Serious security or safety concerns should be escalated privately to the project maintainers.
